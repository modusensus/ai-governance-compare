# Cyber Resilience Act and NIS2

> The EU's security layer. The **Cyber Resilience Act** puts cybersecurity requirements on products with digital elements; **NIS2** puts them on the organisations that run essential and important services. One is about what you ship; the other is about how you operate.

## In one line

CRA makes security a condition of selling digital products in the EU; NIS2 makes risk management and incident reporting a duty for a wide set of entities.

## Cyber Resilience Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/2847

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2024-12-10 | entered into force |
| **2026-09-11** | vulnerability and incident **reporting** obligations apply |
| **2027-12-11** | the full set applies, including CE marking and conformity assessment |

What it does:

- requires manufacturers of products with digital elements to meet essential cybersecurity requirements across the lifecycle — secure by design, vulnerability handling, security updates;
- introduces **conformity assessment and CE marking** for the products in scope;
- imposes **active exploitation and incident reporting** to ENISA and national authorities.

## NIS2 — Directive (EU) 2022/2555

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2023-01-16 | entered into force (twentieth day after the OJ publication of 27 December 2022) |
| 2024-10-17 | deadline for Member States to transpose into national law |

What it does:

- covers **essential and important entities** across many sectors (energy, transport, health, digital infrastructure, public administration, manufacturing, and more);
- requires **risk-management measures**, including **supply-chain security** and management accountability;
- requires **incident notification** within defined deadlines;
- coordinates supervision through national authorities and the Cooperation Group.

## Why this pair matters for AI

- An AI-enabled product sold in the EU can need **AI Act conformity plus CRA conformity** — two different assessment regimes over the same device.
- Supply-chain security under NIS2 reaches the model and data providers a company depends on.
