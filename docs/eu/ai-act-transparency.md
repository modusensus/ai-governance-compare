# AI Act Article 50 — Transparency Obligations

English · [中文](ai-act-transparency-zh.md)

> Article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, **applicable from 2 August 2026**. The EU's rule that AI content must be marked and that people must be told when they are dealing with AI.

## In one line

The AI Act's disclosure clause: in defined situations, providers and deployers must make sure users know they are interacting with an AI system, or that content is AI-generated.

## Who and what it governs

- Duty holders: **providers** and **deployers**.
- Four situations are covered:
  1. **Direct interaction with an AI system** (e.g. chatbots) — users must be informed;
  2. **Synthetic content** — AI-generated audio, image, video and text must carry **machine-readable marking**;
  3. **Deepfakes** — content that is artificially generated or manipulated must be disclosed as such;
  4. **Emotion recognition and biometric categorisation** — affected people must be informed.

## Timeline

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2024-06-13 | regulation published (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) |
| 2026-07-20 | Commission publishes interpretive guidelines on Article 50 |
| 2026-08-02 | Article 50 transparency obligations start to apply |
| 2026-12-02 | deadline for machine-readable marking of generative systems already on the market before 2026-08-02 (a grace period introduced when the AI Act was amended by the Digital Omnibus on AI) |

## Penalties

Breaches of the Article 50 obligations fall under **Article 99(4)** of the AI Act: up to **EUR 15 million** or **3% of total worldwide annual turnover for the preceding financial year**, whichever is higher.

## The 2026 amendment

The AI Act was amended by **Regulation (EU) 2026/1744** (the Digital Omnibus on AI), in force from 27 July 2026. Article 50 itself still applies from 2 August 2026; what the amendment changed is the grace period for machine-readable marking described in the timeline above. See [`ai-act.md`](ai-act.md).

## Comparison with China

- China's counterpart is the Measures for Labelling AI-Generated Synthetic Content plus the mandatory national standard GB 45438-2025, using a dual track of **explicit labelling + implicit labelling**.
