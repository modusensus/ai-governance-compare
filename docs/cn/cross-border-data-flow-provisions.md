# Provisions on Promoting and Regulating Cross-Border Data Flows

> 促进和规范数据跨境流动规定（国家互联网信息办公室令第 16 号）. Published and effective 22 March 2024. It does not replace the three transfer routes — it defines when you do **not** need them, and where the thresholds sit.

## In one line

China's cross-border regime is **flow-level and threshold-driven**: depending on how much personal information leaves the country in a calendar year, you either need nothing, a filed standard contract or certification, or a full security assessment.

## The three routes (from PIPL Article 38)

1. **Security assessment** by the CAC.
2. **Standard contract** with the overseas recipient, filed with the provincial CAC.
3. **Personal information protection certification**.

## The thresholds (from the 2024 Provisions)

| Cumulative volume from 1 January of the year | What is required | Article |
|---|---|---|
| Under **100,000** individuals' personal information, with no sensitive PI and no important data | **Exempt** — no assessment, contract or certification | Art. 5(4) |
| **100,000 to under 1,000,000** individuals' PI (excluding sensitive), **or** under **10,000** individuals' sensitive PI | Standard contract **or** certification | Art. 8 |
| **1,000,000 or more** individuals' PI (excluding sensitive), **or 10,000 or more** individuals' sensitive PI, **or any important data** | **Security assessment** | Art. 7(2) |
| Any transfer of personal information or important data by a **critical information infrastructure operator (CIIO)** | **Security assessment**, whatever the volume | Art. 7(1) |

Three things the table makes easy to miss:

- **Sensitive personal information has no small-volume exemption.** The `under 100,000` exemption is expressly limited to personal information that is *not* sensitive, so a single sensitive record already needs a standard contract or certification.
- **The CIIO rule is about who you are, not how much you move.** Articles 3 and 4 still exempt a CIIO — data carrying no personal information and no important data can always leave — but no other exemption does.
- **A security assessment result is worth more than it used to be**: valid for **3 years**, extendable once by another 3 on application within 60 working days before it expires (Art. 9).

## Exemptions listed in the 2024 Provisions

- **No personal information and no important data** (Art. 3): international trade, cross-border transport, academic cooperation, cross-border manufacturing and marketing.
- **Passed through, not gathered here** (Art. 4): personal information collected and generated abroad, processed in China and sent out again, where the processing introduced no domestic personal information or important data.
- **Necessary for a contract the individual is a party to** (Art. 5(1)): cross-border shopping, delivery, remittance, payment, account opening, flight and hotel booking, visa applications, exam services.
- **Cross-border HR management** (Art. 5(2)): staff personal information sent under lawfully adopted employment rules and a lawfully concluded collective contract.
- **Emergency** (Art. 5(3)): necessary to protect a person's life, health or property.
- **Small volume** (Art. 5(4)): under 100,000 individuals in the calendar year — non-sensitive only, and never important data.

Free trade zones may issue their own **negative lists** (Art. 6): data outside the list needs no route at all.

A further relaxation is easy to miss — you must declare **important data** only if a department or region has told you, or publicly listed it, as important (Art. 2).

## Relationship to other instruments

- **PIPL Article 38** creates the three routes; the 2024 Provisions adjust the thresholds and the exemptions.
- **Measures for Security Assessment of Data Exports** (Order No. 11, published 2022-07-07, effective 2022-09-01) govern the assessment procedure.
- **Measures for the Standard Contract for Export of Personal Information** (Order No. 13, published 2023-02-22, effective 2023-06-01) govern filing and content.
- **Measures for Certification of Personal Information Export** (CAC and SAMR Order No. 20, published 2025-10-14, **effective 2026-01-01**) govern the certification route: https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-10/17/c_1762449728720008.htm — its Article 5 confirms who may use it: a **non-CIIO** exporter moving 100,000–1,000,000 individuals' non-sensitive personal information, or under 10,000 individuals' sensitive personal information, and never important data.
- Where the older measures and the 2024 Provisions disagree, **the 2024 Provisions prevail** (Art. 13).
