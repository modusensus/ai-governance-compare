# Measures for Labelling AI-Generated Synthetic Content

English · [中文](ai-generated-content-labeling-measures-zh.md)

> Adopted and signed by CAC, MIIT, MPS and NRTA on **7 March 2025**, released online 14 March 2025 as the notice **国信办通字〔2025〕2号**; **effective 1 September 2025**. China's most direct rule requiring AI-generated content to be labelled.

## In one line

Text, images, audio, video and virtual scenes produced or synthesised by AI must carry labels: **explicit** labelling (visible to users) and **implicit** labelling (written into file metadata).

## Who and what it governs

- Object: AI-generated synthetic content — text, images, audio, video, virtual scenes.
- Duty holders: service providers (the generating side and distribution platforms).

## Key requirements

- Add **explicit** labels so users can perceive that content is AI-generated.
- Add **implicit** labels in file metadata so platforms and regulators can identify and trace it.
- Providers must not delete, tamper with or forge labels, and must not help others evade labelling.

## What the metadata has to carry (Articles 3, 5 and 6)

The **elements** are named by the measures themselves, not left to the standard:

- **Two kinds of label (Article 3)** — an **explicit label** (显式标识) is added to the content or the interaction interface, presented as text, sound or graphics, and must be one "可以被用户明显感知到"; an **implicit label** (隐式标识) is inserted by technical means into the **file data**, where it is not readily perceptible to the user.
- **Production elements (Article 5)** — acting under Article 16 of the Deep Synthesis Provisions, the provider writes the implicit label into the **file metadata**, which carries the content's **attribute information**, the **provider's name or code**, and a **content identifier**, together described as 制作要素信息. **Digital watermarks are encouraged, not mandated.** The same article defines file metadata as descriptive information embedded in the file header in a specific encoding, recording the file's origin, attributes and purpose.
- **Distribution elements (Article 6)** — a provider that spreads network content must prompt the public wherever it verifies AI generation from the metadata, relies on a user's declaration, or detects generation traces; in those three cases it must **also** write attribute information, **the platform's name or code**, and a content identifier — 传播要素信息 — into the metadata. It must supply the labelling function and remind users to declare.
- **The standard attaches at Article 11** — labelling must additionally satisfy the requirements of the relevant laws, administrative regulations, department rules and **mandatory national standards**. That clause is what makes GB 45438-2025 binding rather than advisory.

## Why it matters

This is where China moved fastest on "AI content must be identifiable": the measures took effect in September 2025, while the EU's comparable transparency duty only starts to apply in August 2026.

## Companion technical standard

- **GB 45438-2025 《网络安全技术 人工智能生成合成内容标识方法》** — a **mandatory national standard** (the portal classifies it 强标), published **2025-02-28**, in force **2025-09-01**, the same day as these measures; status 现行. The official record carries its **own English title**, *Cybersecurity technology—Labeling method for content generated by artificial intelligence*, and lists an English-language version; this card previously gave a translation of its own. Classifications **CCS L80**, **ICS 35.030**. Both the competent department and the technical committee (主管部门／归口部门) are **中央网络安全和信息化委员会办公室** — the cyberspace regulator owns the technical format — while the issuing bodies are 国家市场监督管理总局 and 国家标准化管理委员会.
  - Official record: https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=F32EA2A561F1886CD8D606513512D547
- **What the drafters say it does** — [the Q&A](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654685896173.htm) answers this directly in questions four and five: the measures state the management requirements and deliberately do **not** prescribe implementation, while the standard, issued and commenced together with them, "主要提出强制执行部分的标识具体实施方式和操作方法". Its content is given as three parts — how to add **文字、角标、语音、节奏** style explicit labels to text, images, audio, video and virtual scenes; how to write the **metadata implicit label** into a file; and a **预留字段** in that implicit label for protective information such as 标识完整性 and 内容一致性. The expert commentary adds that the standard fixes parameters such as an explicit label's duration and size and the elements an implicit label must contain. — **B**
- **The field names themselves are not in this repository.** The portal does publish mandatory standards in full, but this one is served only as tiled page images inside a scripted viewer behind a verification step, and every scripted request for its page assets returned an empty body (2026-10-11). The exact metadata keys, their encodings and the per-format parameters therefore remain **unverified**, and nothing in this card beyond the two bullets above is claimed about the standard's interior. ⚠
