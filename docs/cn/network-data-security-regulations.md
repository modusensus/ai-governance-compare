# Regulations on Network Data Security Management

> 网络数据安全管理条例, State Council Order No. 790. Adopted at the State Council executive meeting of 30 August 2024, published 24 September 2024, **effective 1 January 2025**. It is the implementing regulation that ties the Cybersecurity Law, the Data Security Law and the Personal Information Protection Law into one operational rulebook.

## In one line

The regulation that turns China's three data pillars into day-to-day obligations: classify your data, secure the processing lifecycle, handle important data and cross-border flows, and take extra care if you are a large network platform.

## Who and what it governs

- Applies to **network data processing activities** in China, covering both personal information and non-personal data.
- Built on the Cybersecurity Law, the Data Security Law, the Personal Information Protection Law and the Civil Code.

## What it covers

| Area | Substance |
|---|---|
| General duties | Establish data-security management systems, classify and grade data, appoint responsible staff, keep records, and conduct risk assessments. |
| Personal information | Restates and operationalises PIPL duties: notices, consent, separate consent where required, and handling of sensitive information. |
| Important data | Obligations for processors who handle **important data**: designate a person responsible, conduct a security assessment, and report risks. |
| Cross-border transfer | Ties into the three routes under PIPL Article 38 and the 2024 cross-border provisions. |
| Large network platforms | Extra duties for platforms with a large user base — a "gatekeeper"-style provision covering third-party products, public-interest obligations and platform rules. |
| Incidents | Reporting and response obligations when an incident occurs. |
| Government handling | Rules on state authorities accessing and processing network data. |

## Why it matters

- It converts principles into checklists — the instrument a compliance team actually reads, rather than the statutes themselves.
- The large-platform provisions extend Chinese-style platform responsibility into the data-security field, parallel to the algorithmic recommendation provisions.

## Relationship to other instruments

- Parent statutes: Cybersecurity Law (effective 2017-06-01; amended 2025-10-28, in force 2026-01-01, articles renumbered), Data Security Law (effective 2021-09-01), PIPL (effective 2021-11-01).
- Cross-border specifics: PIPL Article 38 and the Provisions on Promoting and Regulating Cross-Border Data Flows (2024-03-22).
- Platform specifics: Algorithmic Recommendation Provisions (2022-03-01).
