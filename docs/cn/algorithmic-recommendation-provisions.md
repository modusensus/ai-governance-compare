# Provisions on the Administration of Algorithmic Recommendations

> 互联网信息服务算法推荐管理规定, Order No. 9 of four departments. Effective 1 March 2022. China's core platform-algorithm rule, read in full from the official text.

## In one line

Whoever pushes information to users by algorithm must take responsibility for the algorithm itself: publish how it works, let users switch it off, file it with the regulator — and never use it to manipulate opinion, addict children, or price-discriminate.

## Who and what it governs

- Applies to providing internet information services in China **by applying recommendation technology** (Article 2).
- Those technologies are defined broadly: **generation and synthesis, personalised push, ranking and selection, search and filtering, scheduling and decision** (Article 2).

## Key obligations

| Article | Obligation |
|---|---|
| 7 | Algorithm safety responsibility: audit mechanisms, ethics review, user registration, publication review, data and personal-information protection, anti-fraud, security assessment, incident response; publish service rules; staff the service properly. |
| 8 | Regularly review and validate the algorithm; **must not build models that induce addiction or over-consumption**. |
| 9 | Maintain a feature library of illegal and harmful information. **Content generated or synthesised by an algorithm must be prominently labelled before it may be transmitted** — the hook into the labelling measures. Illegal information must be stopped and reported. |
| 10 | Manage user models and tags; illegal or harmful keywords must not be recorded as interests or tags. |
| 11 | Ecological management of prominent positions: front page, trending, featured, rankings, pop-ups. |
| 14 | No fake accounts, account trading, account manipulation, fake likes/comments/shares, no information blocking, over-recommendation, ranking manipulation, or control of trending or featured lists. |
| 15 | No unreasonable restrictions on other service providers; no monopoly or unfair competition. |
| 16 | Tell users prominently that recommendation is used, and publish the **basic principles, purpose and main operating mechanism**. |
| 17 | Give users a non-personalised option **or an easy way to switch recommendation off**, and a way to select or delete personal-feature tags. Switching off must take effect immediately. |
| 18 | Minors: age-appropriate modes; no content that could prompt unsafe imitation or bad habits; **no algorithmic inducement into addiction**. |
| 19 | Elderly users: age-friendly services, plus monitoring and handling of telecom and online fraud. |
| 20 | Workers under scheduling algorithms: order allocation, pay structure, working hours, rewards and penalties. |
| 21 | Consumers: **no unreasonable differential treatment in price or other terms** based on preference or trading history. |
| 22 | Accessible complaint and reporting channels with clear process and response deadlines. |

## Filing and supervision

- **Article 24** — a provider **with public-opinion attributes or social-mobilisation capacity** must file through the algorithm filing system **within 10 working days** of starting service, reporting its name, service form, application field, algorithm type, self-assessment report and proposed public disclosure. Changes: within 10 working days. Termination: cancel the filing within 20 working days.
- **Article 25** — the regulator files and publishes a filing number within 30 working days.
- **Article 26** — the filing number must be displayed prominently with a link to the published information.
- **Article 27** — such providers must also undergo a **security assessment**.
- **Article 23** — tiered and classified management based on public-opinion attributes, content category, user scale, data importance and the degree of intervention in user behaviour.

## Penalties

- **Article 31** — for breaches of Articles 7, 8, 9(1), 10, 14, 16, 17, 22, 24, 26: warning, public criticism, order to correct within a deadline; if refused or serious — **suspend information updates and fine RMB 10,000 to 100,000**.
- **Article 32** — for breaches of Articles 6, 9(2), 11, 13, 15, 18, 19, 20, 21, 27, 28(2): handled under the relevant laws and administrative regulations.
- **Article 33** — obtaining a filing by concealment or false materials: revocation, warning, public criticism; serious cases: suspend information updates and fine RMB 10,000 to 100,000.

## Relationship to other instruments

- Built on the Cybersecurity Law, Data Security Law, Personal Information Protection Law and the Measures for the Administration of Internet Information Services (Article 1).
- Article 9 connects directly to the AI content labelling regime.
