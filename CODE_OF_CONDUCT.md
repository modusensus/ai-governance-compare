# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of an open, welcoming and collaborative environment, we pledge that participation in this project is a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Argue about facts, sources and rules — never about people.
- Respect other jurisdictions and other positions. This repository **compares**; it does not take sides.
- Bring sources and accept correction. When someone shows you an error, fix it.
- Write clearly and with restraint.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, belittling, or mocking other contributors.
- Flaming, brigading, ragebait, or political point-scoring.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Harassment, stalking, threats, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and in any public communication made on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

Report violations through the repository's issues or a private channel to the maintainers. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors.
